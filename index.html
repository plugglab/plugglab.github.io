01Getting started

TheHollowing is a slow-burn horror mod. Nothing attacks you on day one. Instead, a hidden fear meter called Dread rises while you play, and the world quietly starts to go wrong.

  1. Install Fabric (with Fabric API) or NeoForge for Minecraft 26.2.
  2. Install GeckoLib for the same loader.
  3. Drop the TheHollowing jar into your mods folder and start a world.

On the first night, listen carefully. Something will let you know it is there.

Tip

Playing with friends halves how fast your Dread grows while they are within 16 blocks. Being alone is always worse.

02Dread & phases

Every player has their own Dread, from 0 to 100. It never shows on screen. You only notice it through what starts happening around you.

010305580

What raises it

  • Simply being in the world, a little every minute.
  • Standing in darkness.
  • Being outside at night, alone.
  • Being deep underground, alone.
  • Every new day you survive.
  • Night terrors and seeing things you should not.

What lowers it

  • Staying in a well-lit room.
  • Standing near a Ward Candle.
  • Sleeping through the night (not on Intense).
  • Calming Tea, Smelling Salts and Flares.
No going back

Once you reach a phase, you never drop below it. You can calm down within a phase, but whatever has found you does not forget.

  1. Quiet0+

    Nothing yet. Enjoy it.

  2. Unease10+

    Sounds that should not be there: footsteps behind you, mining nearby, knocking, creaks, scratching, whispers, a door that opens by itself.

  3. Paranoia30+

    The world gets touched. Torches burn out, blank signs appear, doors are left open, items get renamed, strangers write in chat. The Mimic and the Visitor appear.

  4. Stalked55+

    You are being watched. The Shadow Stalker observes from a distance, the Crawler comes out of caves, the Faceless arrives. A low drone follows you, and sleep turns into night terrors.

  5. Hunted80+

    It stops watching. In the dark, the Shadow Stalker hunts you. Run, ring a bell, or light a flare.

03Monsters

Monsters sent by TheHollowing are directed: they come for one player. The Shadow Stalker and the Faceless cannot be hurt while they are after you. Every one of them can be driven away by light, sound and salt.

Shadow Stalker

  • Stalked
  • Hunted

A tall horned shape that watches you from the edge of your sight. In the Hunted phase it chases you through the dark. The first time it catches you in a night, you are left on half a heart. The second time, you die.

Hollow Bell, Flare, Salt Line, Ward Candle. While hunting, it shrugs off a bell or flare about a third of the time. A Stand-In Effigy saves you from the second catch. Driving it off sometimes drops a Shadow Horn.

Crawler

  • Stalked

A pale, skittering thing that climbs walls and ceilings. You will usually hear it before you see it.

Light. It dissolves in bright places. It can also be killed.

Mimic

  • Paranoia

It looks exactly like you, down to your skin. It repeats what you type in chat back to you, and sometimes it laughs.

Do not trust a player who looks like you.

Visitor

  • Paranoia
  • Night

It knocks on your door at night and begs to be let in. It answers what you say in chat. Do not open the door.

Keep the door shut until morning. A Ward Candle by the door helps.

Lurker

  • Paranoia
  • Your bed

It lives under your bed. Some nights you lie down and hear it breathing: “You can’t sleep, something is under your bed.” Pale fingers reach out from the dark. Get too close without looking and it grabs your ankle.

Look straight at it. It hates being seen and vanishes. Then you can sleep.

Faceless

  • Stalked

Very tall, with no face at all. It only moves when you are not looking. Every time you turn away, it is closer. When it reaches you, everything goes dark.

Stare at it for a few seconds and it vanishes. Daylight also sends it away.

04Events

Events come every few minutes, faster the higher your Dread. They never happen while you are fighting, sleeping, in a menu, or near a Ward Candle.

FromWhat happens
UneaseFootsteps behind you, mining in the walls, a door opening, knocking, creaking wood, scratching beside you, footsteps above you, whispers, strange ambience.
ParanoiaTorches burn out, blank signs appear, doors you closed are left open, an item gets renamed, a stranger writes in chat, someone whispers in chat, glass gets tapped, a music box plays near your bed, a door slams far away, a distant scream. The Mimic and the Visitor.
ParanoiaA whisper right in your ear. Breathing under your bed when you come home at night.
StalkedA fake death message, total silence, the Crawler, a figure watching you, the Faceless, and jumpscares in the dark.
The chosen one

On a server, the scariest events are saved for one player at a time: whoever has the most Dread and is alone.

Jumpscares

When the Shadow Stalker catches you, when the Faceless strikes, during night terrors, and sometimes in the dark, a face fills your screen with a scream. They can be turned off in the settings.

Bad nights

From Paranoia on, beds stop being safe. Something may be under yours when you try to sleep. From Stalked on, night terrors can tear you out of sleep and leave something waiting beside the bed.

Talking to it

Sometimes, if you ask questions in chat, something answers. Try asking who it is.

07Structures

Three kinds of ruins are scattered around the world. Their chests hold journals, flares, salt and other supplies left behind by those who came before.

A camp someone left in a hurry.

A hidden stash of supplies. Someone planned to come back for it.

Hollow Remains

What is left of a place it already visited.

08Settings

Open the settings with the red eye button in the top-left corner of the title screen (or the pause menu, for operators), through Mod Menu on Fabric, or from the Mods list on NeoForge. They are saved in config/thehollowing.json.

SettingWhat it does
IntensityNormal is a slow burn. Intense doubles how fast Dread grows, brings events and hunts more often, and stops sleep from calming you.
Dread SpeedHow fast fear builds, from 0.5x to 3x. Intense doubles it on top.
World EventsLets it open doors, snuff torches and leave signs.
Fake ChatLets strangers who are not there write in chat.
Monsters Can KillWhen off, the Shadow Stalker only ever leaves you on half a heart.
Chase StingPlays a sting when a hunt begins.
JumpscaresSudden faces and screams. Turn this off if you are sensitive to flashing or sudden loud sounds.
On servers

Only the server’s own config counts. Server owners can switch with /thehollowing intensity intense.

The questions it recognises and its answers live in config/thehollowing_chat.json. Use &k for scrambled text and %player% for the player’s name.

09Commands

All commands need operator permission.

CommandWhat it does
/thehollowing intensity [normal|intense]Shows or switches the intensity.
/thehollowing dread <player> [value]Shows or sets a player’s Dread.
/thehollowing event <id> <player>Fires an event right away. Tells you what it needs if it cannot run.
/thehollowing summon <player>Starts a hunt.
/thehollowing reloadReloads both config files.
/thehollowing lootchest set <name>Gives the chest you look at one of the mod’s loot tables.
/thehollowingdebugDread overlay for testing. Operators only.

10FAQ

Nothing is happening. Is it broken?
Probably not. The mod starts slowly on purpose. Try playing a night outside without torches. If you want it faster, switch to Intense.
Something is under my bed and I can’t sleep.
Look at it. Really look. Then try again.
Can I kill the Shadow Stalker?
Not while it is after you. You can only drive it away. Wild ones that are not hunting anyone can be fought.
My torch went out and will not light up again.
That is a Burnt-Out Torch. Craft it with coal or charcoal to get a working torch back.
Can I put it in my modpack?
Yes, as long as you use the official release and credit PluggLab Studio. See the license for the details.
Where do I report a bug?
On the Discord server.
